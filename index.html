AI case file analysis for litigation teams

Legalnaut reads the whole case file, scans and mailboxes included, and gives you the chronology, the contradictions, a proof chart and a dossier for every witness. Each fact cites the document, the page and the quotation it came from.

Open legalnaut.com All features

Upload one archive

A ZIP of the file, a mailbox export or a folder of scans.

Read in minutes

About 100 documents are read, dated and cross-checked in a few minutes.

Every line has a source

One click opens the document on the cited page with the passage highlighted.

Read the file once

Every page goes in once and nothing is analysed twice.

Archives and mailboxes

Upload a ZIP, an .mbox, .pst, .eml or .msg export, or forward mail to the matter's address. Each message is filed with its thread, and each attachment becomes its own document.

OCR to the last page

Scanned bundles are read in full. A document with average OCR confidence below 70% gets a badge, so you check its quotations against the scan.

Chats and recordings

WhatsApp and Messenger screenshots become transcripts of who wrote what and when. Hearings and voice messages are transcribed into searchable text.

E-mail and photo checks

E-mails are checked against their DKIM signature and received chain, photographs against their EXIF data. Each check reports verified, failed or unavailable.

Know what happened

Dates, people and events from every document land on one timeline.

A chronology from the documents

A pleading that describes twelve dated events puts twelve entries on the timeline, each with its quotation. The export flags every gap of ninety days or more.

Who said what about whom

A profile for every person and organisation: their statements, what others said about them, the documents they wrote and the events they took part in.

Correspondence, measured

Who wrote to whom, how often, how long replies took and the longest silences, counted from the mail headers.

Every sum, with its page

Maintenance, wages, invoices, claimed and awarded amounts go into one table with payer, payee and date, and export to XLSX.

Find where the accounts disagree

Statements are split into claims, and every claim is checked against the rest of the file.

Two quotations side by side

A contradiction shows both quotations with their documents and pages and a sentence on why they conflict.

A lawyer confirms

Each pair is a suggestion until you confirm or dismiss it. A dismissed pair never returns.

No self-corroboration

A later letter from the same author does not count as confirming their earlier claim.

The case against you

Devil's advocate writes the strongest opposing argument from your own matter: the weak exhibits and the contradictions that hurt.

Prove it

From issue to exhibit to gap on one chart, and every draft checked against the file.

The proof chart

Each issue you must establish sits next to the exhibits that carry it. An issue with nothing behind it is marked as a gap.

Evidence found as the file grows

Every new document is weighed against every open issue, and each match waits under its issue with the quotation and page.

Citation check

Before you see a draft or a chat answer, Legalnaut confirms the cited document and page exist and the quotation is really there. Anything else is reported.

Bundles and exports

An evidence bundle as one PDF with index, bookmarks and a sha256 annex. A chronology and statement of facts as DOCX, with fixed exhibit numbers.

Prepare the hearing

Witness dossier

Everything the file holds on one person, including the statements the records contradict, each line with document, page and quotation.

Examination outline

Facts that help, facts that hurt, an impeachment sequence for each inconsistency, and the questions the file cannot answer.

Deadlines from the rule

Time limits computed from the provision and the date they run from, with the arithmetic written out and a reminder before each one.

Matter briefing

Posture, what changed, next steps, gaps and risks, written from the matter's own material.

An AI assistant that reads before it answers

Ask about your case in plain words. The assistant searches the file, opens the documents and reads them in full, then answers with a citation on every source.

Four ways to search

Full text with filters, search by meaning across languages, the text of scans and recordings, and documents your team added later.

Counts from the database

A question that needs a number runs a read-only query on the case tables, so no model guesses a total.

Your team and your client in one file

Invite colleagues and clients by e-mail, on every plan, with no limit on users. The interface runs in English, Polish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch and Ukrainian. Evidence stays in its original language, and every view, download and export goes into an audit log.

What Legalnaut will not do

  • It does not give legal advice or decide what a document means.
  • It does not file anything with a court.
  • It is not an e-discovery platform: there is no privilege review.

Try it on a real case

The live demo holds a public case file with its filings already read. It needs no account and no card.
